# PRD-001: OpenPhysio v0.1 for Azhar

Status: Approved (D-1)
Version: 0.1-draft, 2026-11-09
Project/milestone: OpenPhysio / v0.1 dogfood (R1)
Source: product plan https://claude.ai/artifact/P4HHsbmc7kHbwoM9TP52oS and technical plan https://claude.ai/artifact/V5xvMu14HDF2xtRuWDPYyY

## Problem and evidence
Azhar trains across home, gym, pool and road with five goals, sits for long periods, and has past injuries. Plans from scattered sources ignore those injuries, band data is locked in the Chinese Mi Fitness app (no Strava), and exercise how-tos mean searching Google.

## User and triggering situation
Azhar, on his Pixel 8a, deciding what to train today and logging it with minimal effort.

## Desired outcome and success measure
A plan he follows for 2 weeks that respects his injuries, with all sessions logged and progress visible on:
1. Run faster and longer
2. Swim more
3. Jump higher
4. Stronger back for long sitting, more fit overall
5. Gain healthy weight

## Equipment and context
Home: bodyweight, resistance bands, a few dumbbells. Gym: full equipment, no video recording. Pool for swims. Road for runs. Band: Xiaomi Smart Band 10 Pro (China version).

## Included behaviour
Intake with injury history; generated 4-week plan; home/gym logging without video; GPS runs; swims; band data via Gadgetbridge + Health Connect; scanned medical reports and paper logs; AI coach chat with sources; AI-generated exercise demo clips; nutrition targets for weight gain; Learn tab with followed trainers (e.g. Knees Over Toes); Strava export; self-hosted server.

## Explicit exclusions
Other users, iOS, app-store release, live pose coaching, basketball video, paid video libraries, reading Strava data into AI features.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1 Given a fresh install, when I complete intake, then goals, equipment, sports (running, swimming) and injuries with restrictions are saved on the phone and work offline.
- AC-2 Given an injury with a restricted body area, when a plan is generated, an exercise is swapped or the coach answers, then no exercise flagged "avoid if" for that area is suggested and the coach's answer accounts for the injury.
- AC-3 Given my goals, when I generate a plan, then I get a 4-week block shown as a weekly calendar containing 3 runs, 1–2 swims, 2 plyometric sessions with capped foot contacts, back work at least twice, strength on at least 2 days, and a warm-up in every session.
- AC-4 Given my logged history, when a planned run distance, jump count or muscle volume would exceed my biggest in the last 30 days by more than 10%, then the plan caps it.
- AC-5 Given I am at the gym, when I log sets by tap or voice, then reps, load and effort (RPE) are saved without using the camera.
- AC-6 Given I start a run, when I finish it, then route, distance, pace and band heart rate (when available) are saved, and the run can be sent to Strava (API) or exported as a GPX/FIT file.
- AC-7 Given a pool swim recorded on the band or logged manually, then its distance and time appear in my history.
- AC-8 Given Gadgetbridge syncs my band to Health Connect, when I open the app, then steps, heart rate, sleep and workouts since the last sync appear.
- AC-9 Given a scanned medical report or paper training log, when I import it, then extracted injuries or sessions are shown for my approval and only approved items are saved; the original stays on my server.
- AC-10 Given I ask the AI coach a question, then the answer uses my profile, injuries and logs, cites a source for any health claim, and a red-flag symptom produces a stop-and-see-a-professional reply.
- AC-11 Given an exercise in today's session, when I open it, then an approved demo clip plays or a "film it" flag is shown.
- AC-12 Given weight entries, then daily calorie and protein targets are shown (protein 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day) and adjust from the 2-week weight trend.
- AC-13 Given followed trainer channels, then their new videos appear in the Learn tab as embedded YouTube, never copied.
- AC-14 Given Docker on my laptop, when I run the server, then it works without any cloud account and AI keys exist only on the server.

## Failure/empty/edge cases
No band data yet (app works without it); offline at gym/pool (sync later); pain logged mid-session (pain rule swaps or stops the exercise); scan text unreadable (ask for manual entry); no demo clip yet ("film it" flag).

## Constraints
Open source; self-hostable; no paid video subscriptions; Android first; Strava API needs a Strava subscription since June 2026.

## Dependencies
Gadgetbridge support for Band 10 Pro (experimental), Health Connect, AI provider key, HY-Motion licence check for distributing generated clips.
