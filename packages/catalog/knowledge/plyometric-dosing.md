Jumps load tendons and joints hard, so dose them in ground contacts, not just sets. A beginner block might be 30 to 60 contacts per session, two sessions a week, with a day or more between. Land softly on a stable surface, stop when landings get loud or sloppy, and do them early in a session while fresh. Build contacts over weeks. Skip plyometrics with an active knee, ankle or shin problem until a professional clears it. Contact counts here are a coaching convention.
