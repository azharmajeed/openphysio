A resting energy equation from weight, height, age and sex gives a starting estimate of what the body burns at rest. Multiply by an activity factor to get a maintenance guess. It is an estimate and can be off by a few hundred calories either way. Track your weight trend for two to three weeks and adjust from real results, not from the formula.
