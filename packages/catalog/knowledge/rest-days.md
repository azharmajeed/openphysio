---
topic: Rest days and spacing hard sessions
sourceIds: [src-ioc-load-2016]
grade: C
---

Keep hard sessions apart so the same tissue is not stressed on consecutive days. Alternate lower body, upper body and running days, with easy days in between. A rest day can include a gentle walk or mobility. Do not stack plyometrics, long runs and heavy legs on adjacent days. If soreness is still high at the next planned session, shift it by a day.
