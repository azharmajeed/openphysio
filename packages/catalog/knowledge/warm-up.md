A warm-up of five to ten minutes raises temperature and rehearses the movements to come. Start with easy cardio, then mobility for the joints you will use, then a couple of light practice sets of the first exercise. Keep it short and specific. Skip long static stretches before heavy work and save them for after. If a joint feels stiff or sore as you warm up and does not ease, go easier.
