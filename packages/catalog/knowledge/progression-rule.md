---
topic: How to progress exercises
sourceIds: [src-ioc-load-2016]
grade: C
---

Progress one thing at a time: add a rep, add a set, slow the tempo, or add a small amount of weight. Move up when you can finish all sets with good form and the day after feels normal. Keep increases small, around five percent or one to two reps. If pain or form slips, step back to the previous level and build again. Progress is not a straight line.
