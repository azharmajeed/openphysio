Adaptation happens between sessions, so rest counts as training. Aim for about seven to nine hours of sleep and a regular schedule. Poor sleep, high stress or lingering soreness are good reasons to cut a session short or swap hard work for a walk. Plan at least one fully easy day each week. Persistent fatigue, low mood or falling performance over weeks deserves a conversation with a professional.
