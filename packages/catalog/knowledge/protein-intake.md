People who train regularly generally do well on about 1.4 to 2.0 grams of protein per kilogram of bodyweight each day. Spreading it over three to five meals is practical and works fine. Going above that range rarely adds much. Food sources like eggs, dairy, fish, meat, pulses and tofu all count. Anyone with kidney disease or a medical diet should check with a clinician first. This is general nutrition information, not personal advice.
