---
topic: Returning after an ankle sprain
sourceIds: [src-jospt-ankle-2021]
grade: A
---

Guidelines favour early, guided movement over long rest after a sprain. Once walking is tolerable, work on range of motion, calf and foot strength and single-leg balance. Progress from standing balance to uneven surfaces, then hopping and cutting only when pain and swelling are settled. Return to running and sport stepwise. A bad sprain, inability to bear weight or a feeling of instability warrants assessment.
