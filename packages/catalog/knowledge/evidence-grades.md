sourceIds: [src-pedro-2024]

Grade A means a position stand, guideline or consensus from experts reviewing many studies. Grade B means a smaller body of research or a good summary of one. Grade C means a coaching convention or a practical rule with little direct trial evidence. Databases like PEDro rate physio trials so claims can be checked. When the app gives a rule, it tells you the grade and where it came from.
