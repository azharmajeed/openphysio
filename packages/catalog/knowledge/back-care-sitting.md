---
topic: Back care for long sitting
sourceIds: []
grade: C
---

Staying in one position for long stretches tends to stiffen the back and hips, and the best posture is the next one. Stand, walk or change position every 30 to 45 minutes. Short mobility breaks such as hip flexor stretches, gentle back extensions and glute bridges help many people. Build back and core endurance with simple moves like bird dogs and side planks. Back pain with leg numbness, weakness or loss of bladder control needs urgent medical review.
