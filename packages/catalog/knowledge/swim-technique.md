Most gain in swimming comes from position and rhythm, not power. Keep your head neutral, hips near the surface and body long. Reach forward, catch water with a high elbow and push it back past the hip. Breathe out steadily underwater and turn your head to breathe rather than lifting it. Kick from the hips with loose ankles. Use short repeats with rests and focus on one cue per length. Stop if you feel shoulder pinching.
