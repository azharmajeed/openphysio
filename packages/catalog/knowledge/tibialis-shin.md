topic: Tibialis and shin work
sourceIds: []
grade: C
---

The muscle at the front of the shin helps control the foot as it lands and is often neglected in runners. Simple work includes heel walks, toe raises against a wall and slow band dorsiflexion. Do two or three sets of 15 to 25 slow reps on non-running days. Shin soreness that follows a rise in running is usually a load issue, so reduce running first. Pain at one spot on the bone that hurts when pressed or at rest needs a professional look.
