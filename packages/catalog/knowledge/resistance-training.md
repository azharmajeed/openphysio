Strength and muscle grow across a wide range of loads, from fairly heavy to light, as long as sets finish close to hard effort. Two or three sessions a week covering the main movement patterns is enough for most people. Train each muscle group about twice weekly, use two to four sets per exercise, and add reps or weight slowly. Choose movements you can do without pain and with control.
