Gaining muscle needs energy to spare, but a big surplus mostly adds fat. A modest surplus of roughly 10 to 20 percent above maintenance, aiming for about a quarter to half a percent of bodyweight gained per week, is a sensible start. Weigh weekly averages, not single days. If weight is not moving after two to three weeks, nudge intake up a little. If it climbs faster than the range, trim it. Beginners can usually gain faster than experienced lifters.
