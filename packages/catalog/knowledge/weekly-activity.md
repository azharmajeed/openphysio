topic: Weekly activity targets
sourceIds: [src-who-2020]
grade: A
---

For most adults the guidance is 150 to 300 minutes of moderate activity each week, or half that of vigorous activity, plus muscle-strengthening on two or more days. Any movement counts and more is better, with benefits even from small amounts. Break it up however suits your week. People with health conditions or an injury should adapt the targets and check with a clinician.
