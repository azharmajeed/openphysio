Strong quads, hips and calves share the work at the knee. Start with controlled squats to a comfortable depth, step-ups, glute bridges and calf raises. Keep the knee tracking in line with the toes and use a slow lowering phase. Mild discomfort that settles is usually fine, sharp pain, locking, giving way or swelling is not. Progress load slowly over weeks and choose an easier variant first if the knee is sore.
