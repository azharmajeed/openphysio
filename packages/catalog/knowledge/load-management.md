Injuries often follow a mismatch between what the body has been doing and what it is asked to do now. Load means volume, intensity and frequency combined. Raise one thing at a time, keep most weeks similar, and slot an easier week in every third or fourth week. Tissue adapts slowly, so patience beats heroics. If a week felt too hard, repeat it rather than push on. This is general guidance, not a prescription for any one person, and any active injury changes it.
