---
topic: Swap to an easier variant
sourceIds: []
grade: C
---

If pain rises above a mild ache or hangs around into the next day, drop to an easier version of the movement rather than stopping everything. That can mean a smaller range, lighter load, fewer reps, slower tempo or more support. Keep the pattern going so the area stays moving. Retry the harder version only after a few pain-free sessions. If the easier version also hurts, stop that movement and speak to a professional.
