A sudden jump in running, such as a much longer single run or a big weekly total after a quiet spell, is linked to more injuries than gradual build-up. Keep your longest run within a modest step above recent runs and avoid piling a hard week on a gap. After illness or a break, restart lower than you left off. The app flags sessions that jump above your recent pattern. Treat the flag as a prompt to shorten or slow down, not as a diagnosis.
