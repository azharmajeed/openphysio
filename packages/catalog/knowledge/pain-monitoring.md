topic: Pain monitoring during sessions
sourceIds: [src-ioc-load-2016]
grade: C
---

A mild ache that stays low while you train and settles by the next morning is usually acceptable. Pain that climbs during a set, changes how you move, or is still worse the next day is a signal to back off. Rate pain from zero to ten before and after. Sharp, stabbing, catching or giving-way pain is different from an ache and means stop. This is a coaching convention, not a validated clinical threshold, so when unsure choose the cautious option.
